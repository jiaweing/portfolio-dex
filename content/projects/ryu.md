**A privacy-first on-device AI copilot for everyday work.**
Ryu is a desktop AI assistant that runs entirely on your device. It observes your context-screen, audio, and files-to anticipate your needs without ever sending your sensitive data to the cloud.
### AI vs. Privacy
Everyone wants the productivity of AI, but no one wants the security nightmare of sending sensitive data; financial records, proprietary code, private messages to an external black box.
Most "agents" today are just chatbots that wait for commands and live in the cloud, watching your every move.
### Local-First Intelligence
Ryu solves this by processing everything **locally**.
- **It sees what you see:** Ryu understands your screen context to offer relevant help instantly.
- **It hears what you hear:** Real-time meeting transcription and summarization, processed on-device.
- **It keeps your secrets:** Your data never leaves your computer. Period.
