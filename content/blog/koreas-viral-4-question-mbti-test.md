I learnt this from my Korean friends last year. In Korea, MBTI isn't just a personality test, it's a **cultural phenomenon**. Instead of taking the full test (93 questions in the North American version, 88 in the European version), Koreans boiled it down to **4 simple scenarios,** one for each MBTI dimension.
Answer each one, combine your letters, and you've got your type in 30 seconds.
## 1. E or I — Energy
> **"I've been at work from Monday to Friday this week, so I should…"**
<table header-row="true">
<tr>
<td>Response</td>
<td>Type</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Since I've been OUT at work all week, I should **stay in** for the weekend 🏠</td>
<td>**I** (Introvert)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Since I've been at work all week, I should **go out** and spend time outside during the weekend 🎉</td>
<td>**E** (Extravert)</td>
</tr>
</table>
*Introverts recharge by being alone. Extraverts recharge by being around people.*
## 2. S or N — Perception
> **"Imagine you're enjoying a drink at a bar on a Friday night. A horse walks in. What did the horse say to the bartender?"**
<table header-row="true">
<tr>
<td>Response</td>
<td>Type</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>"…Why would a horse enter a bar?" 😐</td>
<td>**S** (Sensing)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>"It probably asked for a drink! Or maybe directions! Or made some weird horse noises?" 🐴</td>
<td>**N** (Intuitive)</td>
</tr>
</table>
*Sensors question the premise — "that doesn't make sense." Intuitives just roll with it and start imagining possibilities.*
## 3. T or F — Decision-making
> **"Your friend says, “I’m feeling so down these days, so I got some bread for myself…"**
<table header-row="true">
<tr>
<td>Response</td>
<td>Type</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>"What kind of bread did you buy?" 🍞 or "Why would buying bread help?"</td>
<td>**T** (Thinking)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>"Oh no, are you okay? What happened? 🥺"</td>
<td>**F** (Feeling)</td>
</tr>
</table>
### The T's thought process, explained:
<callout icon="💭" color="blue_bg">
	1. Wait… you're sad, so you bought *bread*?
	2. Does bread fix sadness?
	3. Hmm, maybe I should try that too
	4. But what kind of bread?
	5. Not all bread can cure sadness, right?
	6. I need to know which bread has this power
	7. I'll buy this bread for them next time they're sad
	**Output:** "무슨 빵 샀는데?" *(What bread did you buy?)*
</callout>
*Thinkers process the logic first. Feelers process the emotion first.*
## 4. J or P — Lifestyle
> **"You're starting a new project at work or school. Your first course of action is…"**
<table header-row="true">
<tr>
<td>Response</td>
<td>Type</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Come up with a detailed plan of steps to take as you progress 📋</td>
<td>**J** (Judging)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Start researching the first thing that comes to mind 🏃</td>
<td>**P** (Perceiving)</td>
</tr>
</table>
*Judgers plan the route before driving. Perceivers start driving and figure it out along the way.*
