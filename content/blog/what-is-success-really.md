# What is success, really?
You define what success is for your life, and not worry what others think about it.
Success is a lousy teacher. It seduces smart people into thinking they can’t lose. Failure is a lousy teacher. It seduces smart people into thinking their decisions were terrible when sometimes they just reflect the unforgiving realities of risk.
Luck plays a role. Leave room for errors when judging.
# Social comparison
The ceiling of social comparison is so high that virtually no one will ever hit it.
# Extremes
The people you see on the news are always on the extremes. They got super lucky building a business or what not. But these will always not be the case. These are outliers. We overlook their success making our actions look like we are doing something wrong.
# The missing piece
What we always see is the finished product, the outcome. We never see the road it took, the losses they incurred for someone to reach there.
Someone’s success looks easy when you’re not the one doing it because the challenges faced by them are invisible to the crowd.
Most things are harder in practice than they are in theory. Not because we are overconfident. But were not good at identifying the price of success, which prevents us from being able to pay it.
Everyone has an incomplete view of the world. But we form a complete narrative to fill in the gaps.
The ability to explain the past gives us the illusion that the world is understandable and the world makes sense. But the world is chaos. The world doesn’t make sense.
