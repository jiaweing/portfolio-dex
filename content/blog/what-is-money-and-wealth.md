# What is money and wealth?
# Financial freedom
Being able to do what you want, when you want, with who you want, for as long as you want, is priceless. It’s the highest dividend money pays and has infinite ROI. Be able to control what you want, what you want to do. The greatest value money gives is giving you control of your time. Time is priceless. But money can buy you time.
# Wealth
Wealth is not something that you can see. We can’t contextualize what we can’t see. Wealth is the nice cars not purchased. The diamonds not bought. The watches not worn, the clothes foregone and the first class ugprade declined. It is the financial assets that have yet to be converted into stuff you see.
Wealth is having options. By not falling into your desires today, to have more tomorrow. Wealth is created by supressing what you could buy today in order to have more stuff and options in the future
# Millionaire
If you want to become a millionaire, are you wanting to spend a million dollars or keeping the million dollars?
# Rich
Rich is having a high recurring income. If you have a stable flow of income that can sustain you a luxurious lifestyle, you are rich.
# It’s hard to copy
Wealth is something you can’t see. People like to learn by imitation. But it’s hard to imitate that. It’s difficult to learn from something you can’t see.
# Savings
Every bit of savings is like taking a point in the future that would have been owned by someone else and giving it back to yourself.
When you don’t have control over your time, you’re forced to accept whatever bad luck is thrown your way. But if you have flexibility you have the time to wait for no-brainer opportunities to fall in your lap. This is a hidden return on your savings.
Having control over your time and options is becoming one of the most valuable currencies in the world.
# Everything has a price
The problem with a lot of things is that the price is not obvious until you experienced them firsthand.
